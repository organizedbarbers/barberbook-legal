Privacy Policy

Barber Book · Effective 11 October 2026 · Română

Barber Book ("the app") is built by Domi Andrei ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. In short: we don't collect your data, we don't run servers, and we don't track you.

1. Information you enter

You can store the following in the app:

  • Your name (typed by you, or provided by Sign in with Apple)
  • Client details: name, phone number and notes
  • Visit records: date, amount, tip, payment method and notes
  • Earnings entries, services and prices, and wage/pay-period settings
  • App preferences such as theme, currency and language

This information is saved on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, it is also synced to your own private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit and iCloud key-value storage, so it can back up and appear on your other devices. We cannot see, access or read this data. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

2. Sign in with Apple

If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple gives the app an anonymous user identifier and, on first sign-in only, your name. These are stored only on your device and in your iCloud account. We don't receive them and we don't use your email address.

3. Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether you have an active purchase so it can unlock features.

4. Your clients' information

If you store details about your clients, you are responsible for having a lawful reason to do so and for handling it in line with the privacy laws that apply to you (for example, the GDPR in the EU). Only store what you need, and delete client records when you no longer need them.

5. What we don't do

  • We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • We don't sell, share or transfer your data to anyone.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.

6. Exporting and deleting your data

You can export your data at any time from Settings → Data. To delete it, delete the records in the app or uninstall the app. To remove the iCloud copy, go to iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage and delete Barber Book's data.

7. Children

Barber Book is a business tool and is not directed at children under 16.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or move your personal data. Because your data stays on your device and in your iCloud account, you can use these rights directly in the app and in iCloud settings. If you have questions, contact us below. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority (in Romania, ANSPDCP at dataprotection.ro).

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.
